    The business idea

    Better plant care, with a helping hand.

    Sprout helps plant owners remember routine care and find temporary help when life takes them away from home.

    01

    Keep care free

    Watering reminders and a simple plant list are the habit-forming core. Ads are a poor first fit: they interrupt a calm tool, need lots of traffic to earn much, and can make care advice feel less trustworthy.

    02

    Earn when a sitter is booked

    A future marketplace could charge a transparent service fee on completed bookings. Owners get convenient help; sitters get local customers. Start in one community and recruit sitters before promising broad availability.

    03

    Build trust before scale

    Real bookings would require identity and reference checks, secure payments, clear cancellation terms, privacy-conscious location sharing, and a plan for damaged plants or missed visits. A paid care-history upgrade could come later if users ask for it.
